# Font Switcher privacy

Updated 13 October 2026.

Font Switcher stores your selected font bytes, its name, and your on/off preference in Chrome's local extension storage. This information is not synced by the extension. Remove font deletes this saved setting and font.

Custom font files are processed on your device and are not uploaded.

When you choose Use Google Font, the extension requests the selected font from Google's font services at fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. Google receives the font request and normal network information such as your IP address and browser request headers. Requests omit credentials and referrer information. Google's handling of these requests is covered by its privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Downloaded fonts are saved locally. Applying a saved font to websites does not make additional Google Fonts requests.

The extension runs on HTTP and HTTPS pages to change text appearance. It does not collect page text, passwords, browsing history, or analytics and has no developer-operated server. It does not sell user data.
