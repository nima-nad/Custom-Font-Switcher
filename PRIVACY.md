# Custom Font Switcher — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026.

## What stays on your device

Custom Font Switcher processes a font file you choose and saves its contents and filename, or a downloaded Google Font and its family name, along with the font source and your on/off preference in Chrome's local extension storage. The extension does not sync this information or upload custom files to a server. No account is needed.

The font remains saved until you replace it, choose Remove font, or uninstall the extension. Switching the font off keeps it saved for later. Remove font deletes the extension's saved font and preference; it does not delete your original file from your computer.

## Google Fonts imports

Google Fonts is optional. Only when you choose Use Google Font does the extension contact fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com over HTTPS to request the chosen font. Google receives the font request, your IP address, and normal browser request headers. The extension omits credentials and referrer information from these requests. It does not send custom font files, website text, or the address of the page you are browsing.

Downloaded fonts are saved on your device. Using a saved font on websites does not trigger further Google Fonts downloads. Google's handling and retention of request data are governed by its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Removing the font locally does not delete any request records held by Google.

## Website access

The extension runs on HTTP and HTTPS pages and matching frames to apply your selected font. It does not read or record page text, form entries, passwords, or browsing history. Website scripts may be able to detect the font or styling applied to their page; this extension does not make changes invisible to the website.

## Data use

The extension has no developer-operated server, analytics, advertising, or tracking service. The developer does not receive your font files or browsing data. User data is not sold or used for advertising, creditworthiness, or lending decisions. Data is used only to provide the font-selection and display features described above. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

## Contact and updates

For privacy questions, use the developer contact shown on this extension's Chrome Web Store listing. This policy will be updated if the extension's data practices change. The date above identifies this revision.
